How to read the graph

A hypothesis is either a claim or a conjecture. It is not a container made of claims. Passages provide evidence for either kind.

Read one chain

  1. Source text: the record from which material was retrieved.
  2. Passage: the quotation and its source reference.
  3. Claim: a statement supported by the passage. Illustrative example: “This phrase occurs in this passage.”
  4. Conjecture: a proposed explanation. Illustrative example: “The shared wording may reflect reuse.” This needs a test, not just a matching quotation.
  5. Research question: the question a claim or conjecture addresses. Several proposals can address the same question.

Claims and conjectures are alternatives in this diagram, not mandatory successive stages. A claim does not turn into a conjecture when accepted.

What the links mean

LinkMeaning
contains / part_ofLocates a passage within its source text.
attestsConnects a supporting passage to a claim or conjecture.
testsConnects a test query to the conjecture it investigates. The existence of a test is not a passing result.
addressesConnects a proposal to the research question it attempts to answer.
parallel_of / descends_fromRecords relationships that reduce the independence of supporting sources.

Checks and decisions

Verified with an exact span means the latest recorded span check located the quotation in its source. It does not verify the interpretation. Repeated checks stay in the passage’s history.

StatusMeaning
ProposedSubmitted for consideration.
AttestedThe required checks for that record type passed.
AcceptedThe researcher approved that record.
RejectedThe researcher rejected that record with a reason.

Accepting a passage does not accept the claims connected to it. Each decision applies to the selected record. A contradiction link is also separate from researcher rejection.

How records appear

Recording a question adds its star. Agent runs can add sources, passages, proposals, queries and checks. Findings lists the claims and conjectures; Graph shows their connections. Corpus searches and Evidence calculations do not add graph records on their own.

The graph can also contain records loaded from earlier work. Opening a session does not rerun the agents.
